**2. Add to Cart instead of Select Options**

This one is deliberate rather than outstanding, so it is worth explaining rather than changing.

The rule across the site is that a product says Select Options when there is a choice to make and Add to Cart when there is not. Garment Decoration Films holds 117 products. 111 of them are stocked in a single width against a single Sage code (106 at 500mm, 2 at 480mm, plus the heat tape and two colour cards), so there is no choice to present. The old "per 25 metre roll" option has become an automatic quantity break instead, so the roll rate is still there and still applies, it just no longer needs a dropdown. The remaining 6 products in that category do have a genuine choice and do say Select Options.

If you would prefer those single width products to go through an options step anyway, we can do that, but as things stand the dropdown would contain one entry.

**Four points that need your confirmation**

1. Optima 471 Gold, 474 Copper and 481 Silver at 630mm. The site had £2.64, Sage has £2.750, and I have gone with Sage. That is a 4.2% rise and it is the only price on the site this work changes. Sage runs two grades in the Optima range: most colours are £2.560 at 630mm and £5.120 at 1260mm, and these three metallics are £2.750 and £5.500. The site was already charging £5.50 for the 1260mm on all three, so only the 630mm was out of step, and it was out of step with its own 1260mm, because half of £5.50 is £2.75 and not £2.64. Four other colours, 402 Anthracite, 412 Yellow, 420 Orange and 485 Green, carried that same £2.64 / £5.50 pairing and were moved onto the Sage rate earlier in the project.

2. Event 100, 101 and 199 at 610mm. The site says £1.47, Sage says £1.410, and I have kept £1.47. 92 Ri-Mark Event colours carry both a 610mm and a 1220mm code in Sage, and in 88 of them the 610mm is exactly half the 1220mm. These three are the only colours where both codes are priced and the halves do not agree: the 1220mm is £2.940, half of that is £1.470, and £1.47 is what the site charges. The fourth and last exception is 366, below. Worth a look at whether the Sage 610mm figure is right.

3. Event 366 Matt Vivid Blue. Sage carries M366610 at £0.000, and I have kept the site's £1.75. Its own 1220mm code is £3.500, half of which is £1.750, and 304 Matt Grey, the same grade of colour, is £1.750 and £3.500. So the zero looks like a gap in Sage rather than a real price. For what it is worth, there are 609 zero priced codes in the export and 1,915 stock codes live on the site, and M366610 is the single point where those two lists meet, so it is an isolated case.

4. F-Sign Platinum 836 AirFlow. The site was offering AirFlow at £3.38 and £6.76 per metre, which are the correct AirFlow rates for the range, but Sage has no P836AF code, so I have not carried it over. 833, 834, 835 and 839 all have AirFlow codes and keep the option, while 837 and 838 never had it.
